## People and organizations
Keep the names and contact details used by customers and other business records.
Choose an existing person when linking a record, or create a Profile first.
Each Profile can have a ProfileType such as person or organization. Types classify
identities; they do not own the people assigned to them.

## Shared identity
Business recipes require this recipe so their name pickers and linked records
use the same Profile and ProfileType models. Changing a name here updates what
linked records display. Access still follows the application's tenant and
permission rules.
