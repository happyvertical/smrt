## Overview

Ingredients are the things you buy in to make what you sell: flour, butter,
steel, fabric. They are products of the material kind, so they have the same
name, price and description as anything you sell, and they are listed apart from
the products you sell. Count them with inventory like any other item.

## Tasks

### Add an ingredient

1. Create a new ingredient.
2. Give it a **{field:name}** that includes the pack size, for example "Bread flour, 25 kg".
3. Enter what you pay in the **{field:price}**, in minor units (cents).
4. Add a **{field:description}** such as the brand or grade.
5. Save. It appears in the ingredients list, not in the products you sell.

### Update what you pay

1. Find the ingredient.
2. Change the **{field:price}** and save.
