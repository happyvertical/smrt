## Overview

Leases are agreements where a customer rents something from you for a period.
Each lease keeps its own line items and dates.

## Tasks

### Record a lease

1. Open **Leases** and choose **New**.
2. Pick the **{field:customerId}** renting from you.
3. Set the **{field:issueDate}** it starts and the **{field:expiryDate}** it ends.
4. Add a line item for each thing rented, then save.

### Keep an eye on it

1. Update **{field:status}** as the lease goes from draft to accepted to
   completed.
2. Check **{field:expiryDate}** to see which leases end soon.
