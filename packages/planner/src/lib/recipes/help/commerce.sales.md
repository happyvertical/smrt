## Overview

Sales is where you take orders from your customers and see where each one
stands. Every order belongs to one of your customers, carries its amounts and
dates, and keeps a status so you always know what is still open.

## Tasks

### Take an order

1. Choose the **New** button above the list.
2. Pick the **{field:customerId}** the order is for. They have to be in
   Customers first.
3. Enter the **{field:subtotal}** and **{field:taxAmount}**, then the
   **{field:totalAmount}** the customer will pay.
4. Set the **{field:issueDate}**, and the **{field:dueDate}** if you expect
   payment by a certain day.
5. Add your own **{field:reference}**, such as a purchase order number the
   customer gave you, so you can find the order later.
6. Save.

### Follow an order through to the end

1. Open the order from the list and choose Edit.
2. Update **{field:status}** as the order moves along, for example from draft to
   accepted to completed.
3. Check the **{field:dueDate}** against today to spot orders that are overdue.
4. Use **{field:notes}** to record calls, changes and anything promised.

### Make a quote with an expiry

1. Create the order as usual and set its **{field:status}** to show it is still
   a draft.
2. Set **{field:expiryDate}** to the last day the price is good for.
3. Put your conditions, such as deposits or delivery, in **{field:terms}**.
