## Overview

Team Chat keeps conversations in rooms so people can find the right discussion,
follow threads, and see reactions without mixing unrelated work together. The
room and message lists are read-only generated views. Create rooms, manage
members, and send messages through the app's authorized chat workflow.

## Tasks

### Find the right room

1. Open **Chat Rooms** from the navigation.
2. Search for a room by its **{field:ChatRoom.name}**, or use its
   **{field:ChatRoom.topic}** to tell similar rooms apart.
3. Open the room and check **{field:ChatRoom.lastMessageAt}** to see where the
   most recent discussion happened.

### Follow a conversation

1. Read the room messages and open a thread when you need the discussion in
   one place.
2. Use the thread **{field:ChatThread.title}** to describe the subject when
   your app supplies one.
3. Add a reaction by choosing the **{field:ChatReaction.emoji}** that best
   communicates your response.

### Keep a room useful

1. Give the room a clear **{field:ChatRoom.name}** and a short
   **{field:ChatRoom.description}**.
2. Set **{field:ChatRoom.roomType}** deliberately: public for broad team
   discussion, private for restricted groups, and direct messages for one-to-one
   conversations.
3. Use a focused **{field:ChatMessage.content}** so teammates can scan the
   conversation and reply in context.
