## Overview

Licence sales record selling the right to use something, such as software or
content, rather than a physical item.

## Tasks

### Record a licence sale

1. Open **Licence sales** and choose **New**.
2. Pick the **{field:customerId}** who bought the licence.
3. Add a line item for what is licensed and check the **{field:totalAmount}**.
4. Set the **{field:expiryDate}** if the licence runs out, then save.

### Follow it

1. Update **{field:status}** when the customer accepts or the sale completes.
