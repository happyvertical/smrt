## Overview

Expenses are the costs the business pays: a receipt, who paid it and what it
was for. Each expense is reviewed once, and a reimbursable one is marked when
the person is paid back.

## Tasks

### Record an expense

1. Create a new expense and write a short **{field:description}**.
2. Enter the **{field:amount}** and its **{field:currency}**.
3. Set the **{field:incurredOn}** date from the receipt and pick a
   **{field:category}**.
4. Choose who **{field:paidBy}**: the company, a person or a third party.
   Tick **{field:reimbursable}** when a person is owed the money back.

### Review it

1. Open the expense and check the receipt attached to it.
2. **{field:reviewStatus}** moves from unreviewed to reviewed, or to rejected,
   when someone reviews it. A rejected expense can be reopened.
