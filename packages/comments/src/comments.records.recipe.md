## Overview

Record comments keep a discussion next to the work it concerns. Open a record
you can access to read its discussion or add a comment. Comments are part of
the record page, rather than a separate list of every discussion.

## Tasks

### Add a comment

Open the record, find Comments, write your message and choose Post comment.
Your app controls who can read the record and who can contribute to its discussion.

### Mention someone

If your app provides mention selection, choose a teammate who can access the
record. When mention notifications are connected, that person receives a
notification. Typing a name alone does not select a recipient.

### Check a failed post

If posting reports an error, refresh the discussion before trying again. A
comment may have been saved even when its mention notification could not be
delivered. If it appears in the discussion, do not post it a second time.
