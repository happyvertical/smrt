## Overview

Bookkeeping keeps your accounts. You list the accounts you use (your chart of
accounts) and record each transaction as a journal whose entries debit and
credit those accounts.

## Tasks

### Set up your accounts

1. Open **Accounts** and choose **New**.
2. Give it a **{field:number}** and a **{field:name}**.
3. Choose its **{field:type}**: asset, liability, equity, revenue or expense.

### Record a transaction

1. Open **Journals** and choose **New**, and add a **{field:description}**.
2. Add entries: pick the account on each and enter a **{field:debit}** or a
   **{field:credit}**. They must add up to the same total.
3. Set the journal's **{field:status}** to posted when it is final. A posted
   journal is voided, not edited.
