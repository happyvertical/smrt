## Overview

Vendors is your list of the suppliers you buy from. Each record holds who they
are, how to reach them and the terms you have agreed, so you can order from them
quickly and keep track of what you have bought.

## Tasks

### Add a vendor

1. Choose the **New** button above the list.
2. Enter **{field:defaultContactEmail}** and **{field:defaultContactPhone}** for
   the person you normally deal with.
3. Set the **{field:currency}** they bill you in and your **{field:paymentTerms}**,
   for example "Net 30".
4. Save. The vendor is now on your list and ready to use on a purchase order.

### Note how a vendor likes to work

1. Open the vendor from the list and choose Edit.
2. Enter **{field:leadTimeDays}**, the number of days they usually need between
   your order and delivery, so you can plan ahead.
3. Enter **{field:minimumOrderAmount}** if they will not accept small orders.
4. Use **{field:notes}** for anything else worth remembering, such as the best
   time to call.

### Keep payment details in one place

1. Open the vendor and choose Edit.
2. Record where payments to this vendor should be sent, so it is all in one
   place.
3. Set **{field:status}** to show whether you still buy from them: active,
   inactive or suspended.
