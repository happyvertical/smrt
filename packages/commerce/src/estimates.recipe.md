## Overview

Estimates are price quotes you send before a customer commits. Each one belongs
to a customer, lists its line items and shows how long the price is good for.

## Tasks

### Write an estimate

1. Choose **New** above the list.
2. Pick the **{field:customerId}** the quote is for.
3. Add the line items, then check the **{field:totalAmount}**.
4. Set **{field:expiryDate}** to the last day the price is good for.
5. Put your conditions in **{field:terms}** and save.

### Follow it up

1. Set **{field:status}** to sent once it goes out.
2. Move it to accepted or declined when the customer answers.
