## Overview

Events is your calendar. Each event has a time and a type, can belong to a
series, and lists who takes part.

## Tasks

### Schedule an event

1. Open **Events** and choose **New**.
2. Enter the **{field:name}**, the **{field:startDate}** and the
   **{field:endDate}**.
3. Pick the **{field:typeId}**, and the **{field:seriesId}** if it repeats.
4. Add the people taking part with their **{field:role}**.

### Keep it current

1. Update **{field:status}** as the event goes ahead, finishes or is postponed.
