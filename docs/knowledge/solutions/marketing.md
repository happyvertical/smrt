Reduce repeated campaign preparation while retaining approval over published material.

# Proposed composition

Represent campaigns, prepare content, schedule work and collect supported performance information. **This is a proposed recipe, not an existing turnkey product or customer result.**

# Work still required

Connect the intended channels, permissions and analytics providers; define brand guidance, review and publication rules. No advertising return or cross-platform coverage is implied.

# Deployment boundary

Publishing to social networks or retrieving their metrics requires those services and connectivity. See offline deployment (documented in happyvertical.com, not in this repository).

# Discovery question

Which channels do you update repeatedly, and who approves each post?
