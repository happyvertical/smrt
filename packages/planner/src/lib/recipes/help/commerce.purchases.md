## Overview

Purchases is where you order from your vendors and keep track of what you have
bought. Every purchase order belongs to one of your vendors and records the
amounts, the dates and where the order stands.

## Tasks

### Place a purchase order

1. Choose the **New** button above the list.
2. Pick the **{field:vendorId}** you are buying from. They have to be in
   Vendors first.
3. Enter the **{field:subtotal}** and **{field:taxAmount}**, then the
   **{field:totalAmount}** you expect to pay.
4. Set the **{field:issueDate}**, and the **{field:dueDate}** for when the
   goods or the payment are due.
5. Add your own **{field:reference}**, such as the vendor's quote number, so
   you can match it to their paperwork.
6. Save.

### Follow an order until it arrives

1. Open the purchase order from the list and choose Edit.
2. Update **{field:status}** as the order moves along, for example from draft to
   accepted to completed.
3. Use **{field:notes}** to record what the vendor told you, such as delays or
   partial shipments.

### Record the agreement

1. Open the purchase order and choose Edit.
2. Write down the **{field:terms}** you agreed with the vendor, such as payment
   or delivery conditions.
3. Set **{field:expiryDate}** if the vendor's offer is only good until a certain
   day.
