# Reports

Ask the assistant for a total, comparison, or trend. Review the proposed report
and approve it to save it. Your saved reports are shared with your team; each
person sees results using their own access to the underlying information.

Reports run fresh when opened. You can archive a report to hide it from the
active list and restore it later. Creating and restoring reports needs report
writing access. Reports need a connected server; the smrt planner previews them over its
sample records instead. Scheduling, renaming, and exporting reports are not available yet.
