# Solutions

Start with the [capability map](/solutions/capabilities.md), then open the recipe for the business need. Recipes are proposed compositions of existing [components](/components/index.md), not finished products; each lists the integration work still required. Evidence labels and refresh rules are in [maintenance](/maintenance.md).

* [Capability map](/solutions/capabilities.md) - Business needs mapped to the component families already visible in source.
* [Customer intake and follow-up](/solutions/customer-followup.md) - Capture enquiries, maintain customer records, and prepare follow-up work.
* [Document intake and reusable knowledge](/solutions/documents.md) - Reduce repeated reading and rekeying of business documents.
* [Marketing preparation and publishing](/solutions/marketing.md) - Reduce repeated campaign preparation while retaining approval over published material.
* [Member or customer portal](/solutions/membership.md) - Assemble an authenticated portal with roles and optional subscription entitlements.
* [Recurring operational reports](/solutions/operations-report.md) - Prepare recurring views of business activity without rebuilding the report each time.
* [Products and stock operations](/solutions/stock.md) - Keep product and stock information together and support recurring operational tasks.
* [Support intake and triage](/solutions/support.md) - Organize requests and help staff focus on cases needing judgment.
* [Time, receipts and job costs](/solutions/time-and-expenses.md) - Reduce repeated entry of time and expenses while keeping review explicit.
* [Business website and content workflow](/solutions/website-content.md) - Publish useful business information through an application composed from existing modules.
