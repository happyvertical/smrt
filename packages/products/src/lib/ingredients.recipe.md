## Overview

Ingredients are the things you buy in to make what you sell: flour, butter,
steel, fabric. They are kept as products of the material kind, so they have the
same name, price and description as anything you sell, and they get their own
list, apart from the products you sell. Count them under Stock like any other
item.

## Tasks

### Add an ingredient

1. In the menu, choose **Ingredients**, then **New ingredient**.
2. Give it a **{field:name}** that includes the pack size, for example "Bread flour, 25 kg".
3. Enter what you pay in the **{field:price}**, in dollars and cents.
4. Add a **{field:description}** such as the brand or grade.
5. Save. It appears on the Ingredients list, not in Products.

### Update what you pay

1. Open **Ingredients** and find the item.
2. Choose Edit, change the **{field:price}** and save.
