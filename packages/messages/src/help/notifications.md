## Notifications
Open the notification bell to see updates addressed to you. Unread updates have a visible marker.
Choose Mark read for one update or Mark all read for every unread update in your authorized accounts.
Follow a notification link to open its related record when one is available.

## Availability
Notifications are scoped to the signed-in person. Your application connects the notification provider and may enable live updates. If loading fails, try again after the connection returns.
