## Overview

Simple products are things you sell that come in one form: one name, one price,
one description. Keep a record for each so you can see everything you offer in
one list, with the price next to it, and change it whenever it moves.

## Tasks

### Add a simple product

1. Create a new product.
2. Give it a **{field:name}**, for example "Ceramic mug".
3. Enter the **{field:price}** in minor units (cents).
4. Add a **{field:description}** so anyone reading the list knows what it is.
5. Save. The product is now on your list.

### Change a price

1. Find the product.
2. Change the **{field:price}** and save.
